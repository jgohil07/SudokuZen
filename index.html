Sudoku, without the clutter

Sudoku Zen is a free online Sudoku game that runs entirely in your browser. There is no sign-up, no account and no advertising. Choose a difficulty, and a fresh grid is generated on your own device in a few milliseconds. Your puzzle, your pencil marks and your statistics stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Every puzzle is checked to have exactly one solution. The generator removes one clue at a time and re-solves the grid after each removal, keeping the removal only when a single solution still remains. That matters: a puzzle with two solutions can be filled in correctly and still be marked wrong.

How to play

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. The digits printed when the puzzle starts are fixed and cannot be changed.

  • Scan for forced cells. Find an empty cell where eight of the nine digits already appear in its row, column or box. Only one digit can go there.
  • Look for hidden singles. Take a digit and a box. If only one empty cell in that box can legally hold it, it belongs there — even if that cell still has several candidates.
  • Use pencil marks. Jot the possible digits into each empty cell. As you place numbers, candidates vanish and forced cells surface on their own.
  • Work the tight units. Rows, columns and boxes that are nearly full constrain the remaining digits the most, so they break open first.

Questions

Is Sudoku Zen free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no account, no payment and no advertising. The whole game runs in your browser.

Does every puzzle have exactly one solution?

Yes. Every puzzle is generated by removing one cell at a time and checking after each removal that exactly one solution remains. A puzzle is only served once that check passes.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your current puzzle, notes, timer and statistics are stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. When you come back, the game asks whether you want to resume or start fresh.

Can I play offline?

Yes. After your first visit the game is cached by a service worker, so it keeps working without a connection. Puzzles are generated on your device rather than downloaded.

What is the daily puzzle?

One puzzle per difficulty per day, derived from the date itself, so everybody who plays the daily puzzle on a given day gets exactly the same grid.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, including landscape, and it can be added to your home screen to run like an app.
